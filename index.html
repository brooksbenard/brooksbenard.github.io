Brooks Benard, Ph.D.

Computational Cancer Biologist · Stanford University

I am a computational biologist in the Department of Pathology at Stanford University. My work focuses on developing and applying statistical learning methods to identify therapeutically relevant targets from gene expression data across bulk, single-cell, and spatial modalities in cancer.

I performed my PhD research with Ravi Majeti in the Cancer Biology program at Stanford University. Prior to Stanford, I completed my bachelor's degree in Molecular Biosciences and Biotechnology at ASU, where I spent time in the labs of Dr. Shelley Haydel at the Biodesign Institute, and Dr. Jonathan Keats at TGen.
